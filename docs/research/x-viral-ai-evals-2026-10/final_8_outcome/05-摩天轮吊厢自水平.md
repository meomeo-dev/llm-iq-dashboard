# 05 摩天轮吊厢自水平（Ferris Wheel Cabin Leveling）

- 建议 ID：`animated-ferris-leveling-v1`
- 英文名：Animated Ferris Wheel with Self-Leveling Cabins

- 公开题面要点：主轮、中心轴承、支架、驱动齿轮组、8–12 个吊厢、反向自水平、连杆与齿轮转向必须正确，输出 HTML。

## 讨论焦点

2026 年 10 月的动画教程反复用摩天轮当例子。社区把难度从“轮子在转”推到“吊厢地板保持水平，而且驱动齿轮反向啮合”。模型常把吊厢焊在轮上，过顶点后人头朝下；或大小齿轮同向转。

典型失败：

- 吊厢随轮翻转。
- 反向旋转加在错误的坐标系，吊厢公转停住。
- 小齿轮与大齿轮同向。
- 吊厢数量不是 8 的整数位置，间距不均。
- 用脚本计算角度。

## 考点

嵌套反向角、齿轮啮合转向、圆周等分、声明式循环。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of a generic Ferris wheel.
SMIL or one inline <style> only. No <script>, no external asset,
no text, no brand.

Structure: an A-frame, one axle, one large wheel, 12 radial spokes,
a toothed bull gear on the wheel, and a smaller drive pinion below
it. Hang exactly 8 cabins at 45-degree spacing. Each cabin is a
rectangle with a visible floor edge and one pivot.

One 12-second seamless loop:
1. The wheel and bull gear rotate clockwise at constant speed.
2. The pinion rotates counterclockwise. Its angular speed is
   4 times the wheel, matching a 4:1 tooth ratio. Teeth stay in
   mesh; do not phase-slide the pinion away from the bull gear.
3. Each cabin translates with the rim but its floor stays horizontal.
   Implement this as an inner rotation equal and opposite to the
   wheel rotation, about that cabin's own pivot.
4. Cabins never intersect the spokes or the frame.
5. The pose at 0s and 12s is identical.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 8 个吊厢等分；任意时刻地板与世界水平线夹角小于 5°；小齿轮转向与大轮相反且角速度约为 4 倍；首尾闭合；最大帧差不低于 0.015；无脚本。

**Partial：** 吊厢保持水平，但齿轮同向、缺齿或传动比明显不是 4。

**Fail：** 吊厢翻转超过 20°；吊厢不随轮公转；出现脚本；轮本身不转。
