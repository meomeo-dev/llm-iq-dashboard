## 讨论焦点

Anatomy 在 10 月 10 日把“一句话变机器”推成当月最大的视觉智能体帖。差速器是 48 小时内出现的社区机构图，考点比机柜和相机更独立：两个输出轴转速必须相反于平均分配。

典型失败：

- 左右轮同速旋转，差速器变成一根通轴。
- 行星齿轮（spider gears）只是装饰，不自转。
- 外侧轮更慢，转弯方向反了。
- 壳体、齿圈和半轴穿插，画家算法失败。
- 输出带交互脚本和轨道控制器。

与已收录相机爆炸图的差别：零件沿固定壳体做啮合旋转，不沿光轴炸开再装回。

## 考点

等轴测壳体、齿圈与行星轮啮合、左右半轴速度差、过弯路径长度。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of a generic open
differential with the housing cut away on the near side. Use a 30°
isometric projection: screenX = (x - y) * cos(30°),
screenY = (x + y) * sin(30°) - z. Hairline technical style:
near-black metal, 1px edges, one muted accent. SMIL or inline CSS
only. No <script>, no brand, no words.

Parts, back to front: input pinion, ring gear, carrier, two spider
gears on a cross pin, two side gears, left axle, right axle, and
two wheels. The housing outline stays closed.

One 4-second seamless loop of a left turn:
1. The pinion turns at constant speed and meshes with the ring.
2. The carrier, cross pin, and ring share that average rotation.
3. Spider gears spin about the cross pin.
4. The right wheel, on the outside of the turn, rotates faster
   than the carrier. The left wheel rotates slower by the same
   amount. Their average equals the carrier speed.
5. A short ground arc under the wheels shows the right arc longer
   than the left arc.
6. No part drifts out of mesh. First and last frames match.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 输入小齿轮、齿圈、十字轴、两只行星轮、两只半轴齿轮和两轮都可辨；右轮角速度大于左轮，二者平均等于齿圈；行星轮相对十字轴有自转；等轴测三轴方向一致；首尾闭合；最大帧差不低于 0.020；无脚本。

**Partial：** 左右速度差正确，但行星轮不转，或投影退化成正视图。

**Fail：** 两轮同速；外侧轮更慢；零件散成爆炸图；出现脚本或品牌标识。
