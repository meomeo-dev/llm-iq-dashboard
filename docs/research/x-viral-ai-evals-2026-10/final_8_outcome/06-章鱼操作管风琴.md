静态替代集本身早于窗口中心。本题只收第 1 题，并加上键与踏板的周期动作，使它和库内静态鹈鹕、动态骑行题分开。Gally 的其余 29 句不入库。

## 讨论焦点

HN 评论认为鹈鹕车已被训练数据饱和。替代句把生物计数和一件复杂机器绑在一起。评论指出 Gemini 3.8 Flash 颜色满、结构散，出现多余颈部和接不上的物体；Qwen 成本低但仍会让章鱼不面向乐器。

典型失败：

- 腕数不是 8，常见 6、9 或融成一团。
- 腕停在空中，没有压键或踏板。
- 管风琴退化成钢琴或一排相同矩形。
- 音管数量与键盘错位。
- 动画只让章鱼上下跳，键不凹陷。

## 考点

腕数守恒、手键盘与脚键盘分工、按键位移、音管高度序列。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of an octopus seated at a
pipe organ, front three-quarter view. SMIL or inline CSS only.
No <script>, no external asset, no text, no brand.

The octopus has exactly 8 tentacles and 2 eyes, and faces the organ.
The organ has 9 pipes in one rank, strictly increasing then
decreasing in height so the longest pipe is near the middle, one
manual of 12 white keys, and one pedalboard of 5 pedals.

One 4-second seamless loop:
1. Four tentacles stay on the manual. Two of them press keys: a
   pressed key moves down and returns. The other four tentacles
   divide as two on the pedalboard and two on the stop knobs beside
   the console.
2. A pressed pedal tilts down and returns. Pedal motion is out of
   phase with the manual.
3. No tentacle splits, merges, or disappears between frames.
4. The body stays on the bench. Tentacles connect to the mantle
   at all times.
5. First and last frames match, and at least one key is in motion
   during the cycle.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 每一帧腕数都是 8；至少两腕接触键盘、两腕接触踏板；键与踏板有可见位移且相位不同；9 根音管中间最高；首尾闭合；最大帧差不低于 0.015；无脚本。

**Partial：** 计数和接触正确，但键不移动，只是腕在滑动。

**Fail：** 任一帧腕数不是 8；章鱼不面向风琴；没有踏板；出现脚本。
