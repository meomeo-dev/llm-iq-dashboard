## 讨论焦点

10 月末的拆解帖证明了“一个数驱动全部零件”。蜈蚣帖把这个数写成步态相位。小模型常把多腿画成同一角度的梳子。

典型失败：

- 所有腿同相，像一把开合的梳子。
- 相位差随意，数不出 0.11。
- 波从尾传到头。
- 身体是刚体直线，没有横向行波。
- 腿数被画成 8、10 或“很多”但不可数。

## 考点

后向变时步态（metachronal wave）、固定相位差、体轴横向波与腿相位锁定。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of a generic robotic
centipede, side view, walking to the right. SMIL or inline CSS only.
No <script>, no external asset, no text.

Body: a chain of exactly 16 segments. Each segment has exactly one
pair of legs, 32 legs total. Legs on the far side may be shorter
and lighter, but every pair must be countable. Each leg has a hip
and a foot.

One 3.2-second seamless loop:
1. Number pairs 0 through 15 from head to tail.
2. Pair k steps at phase k * 0.11 of the cycle, so the wave travels
   from head toward tail.
3. A stepping leg lifts and advances; a planted foot does not slide.
4. The body is an S-shaped rest pose plus a sideways traveling wave
   whose peak moves tailward in step with the leg wave.
5. At least 8 feet are planted at every instant.
6. The first and last frame match exactly.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 16 节、32 腿可数；相邻对的相位差为 0.09–0.13 周期；波向与头尾方向相反；体轴有同向横向位移；支撑足不滑；首尾闭合；最大帧差不低于 0.015；无脚本。

**Partial：** 腿数与后向波正确，但相位差落到 0.05–0.20 之外，或身体没有横向波。

**Fail：** 腿数不可数或不等于 32；全腿同相；波向头端；用脚本逐帧摆腿。
