## 讨论焦点

10 月模型发布帖仍在用鹈鹕车，但作者改用孔雀，因为尾屏眼斑是可数纹理，踏板相位仍保留骑行考点。2026-10-26 对 GPT 6.1 Terra 的记录写明：车、路和树在动，孔雀像焊在车上，腿不蹬，身体几乎无反应，还把水印放错位置。

同系列还测过 Opus 5.5、Gemini 4 变体、Kimi 与 DeepSeek。常见失败是眼斑变成散点、尾屏不跟着骨盆、轮在转而曲柄停住。

与鹈鹕车的差别：不考喙囊和翼手，改考眼斑计数、尾屏铰链和冠羽。不要求水印。

## 考点

眼斑阵列、尾屏随髋关节、曲柄—轮相位、接触约束。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of a peacock riding a
bicycle, side view, facing right. SMIL or inline CSS only.
No <script>, no external asset, no signature, no text.

The peacock has one crest, two legs, one beak, and a folded train
of exactly 12 eye spots arranged in 3 rows of 4. The bicycle has
two equal wheels, a frame, a saddle, handlebars, two pedals, and
a crank.

One 2-second seamless loop:
1. Both wheels rotate at the same speed. The crank rotates with
   them: one wheel revolution equals one crank revolution.
2. The two legs alternate and each foot stays on its pedal.
3. The pelvis rocks with the pedals. The train hinges at the pelvis
   and lags the rock by a small angle. Eye spots stay attached to
   the train and do not change count.
4. The crest bobs with the body. Beak and wings are present but
   do not become extra legs.
5. The bird contacts the saddle throughout. First and last frames
   match.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 眼斑恒为 12 且成 3×4；双脚锁在踏板上；轮与曲柄同周期；尾屏相对身体有滞后摆动；鞍座接触不断；首尾闭合；最大帧差不低于 0.015；无脚本、无签名。

**Partial：** 骑行相位正确，但眼斑数量在 8–16 且不成阵列，或尾屏是刚体不滞后。

**Fail：** 鸟浮在车上；腿不随踏板；轮转曲柄不转；眼斑不可数；出现脚本或水印文字。
