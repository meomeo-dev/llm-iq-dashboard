原帖把 Theo Jansen 步行机构做成可拆的 Three.js 模拟：一根曲柄带动 20 条腿，关节由圆圆交点求出。本草案只保留平面机构学，改成两条反相腿的纯内联 SVG，去掉技能、三维引擎和脚本。

## 讨论焦点

帖子走红是因为一条参考图加一段机构描述，就能得到可拆、可走、仪表与几何同源的模拟。社区接着用同一方法做跳蛛、蝎子和蜈蚣。

典型失败：

- Claude / GPT 用 `<script>` 逐帧解算，违反纯声明式动画。
- 腿被画成贝塞尔“走路姿势”，杆长随帧变化。
- 足端没有约 155° 平地相，变成圆周或椭圆。
- 两条腿同相，身体上下窜而不是交替支撑。

## 考点

定长连杆、圆圆交点装配模式、平地支撑相、反相双腿。不是四足动物步态，也不是已收录的犰狳对角小跑。

## 提示词草案

```text
Generate one self-contained animated SVG of a planar Jansen walking
linkage, side view, on a sand-colored ground line. Use only SMIL
<animate>, <animateTransform>, or one inline <style> with @keyframes.
No <script>, no external file, no font, no bitmap.

Geometry, in the same arbitrary unit, then scale to fit viewBox
"0 0 900 520":
fixed span a=38; vertical offset l=7.8; crank m=15;
rod lengths b=41.5, c=39.3, d=40.1, e=55.8, f=39.4,
g=36.7, h=65.7, i=49, j=50, k=61.9.
Draw two fixed pivots, one crank, and two legs 180 degrees out of
phase. Each leg is a closed chain of those rods. Joints are small
circles. Rods are round-capped strokes. The foot is a larger joint.

Motion, one 4-second seamless loop:
1. The crank rotates at constant speed about its fixed pivot.
2. Every other joint is the rigid-circle intersection of its two
   rod lengths. Stroke length must not change during the cycle.
3. The foot path has a long lower stance of about 155 degrees of
   crank angle, nearly horizontal, then a shorter lift.
4. While one foot is in stance, the other is in lift.
5. At t=0 and t=4s the pose is identical. Peak motion must be visible.
6. Do not draw a brand name, a beach-beast mascot, or explanatory text.
```

## 判读

**Pass：** 11 个命名长度都能在图上对应到杆；抽帧测得杆长变化小于 3%；足端轨迹下沿近似水平且约占曲柄 140°–170°；双腿相位差 160°–200°；首尾帧 MAE 为 0，周期内最大帧差不低于 0.015；无脚本。

**Partial：** 杆数与反相正确，但平地相短于 120°，或只画出一条腿。

**Fail：** 出现 `<script>`；杆长明显伸缩；足端画成圆；双腿同相；用矩形腿模仿动物。
